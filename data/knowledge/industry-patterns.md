# Cross-Industry Reference Patterns

Reference knowledge only. These are *generic patterns observed across common
industries*, provided as retrieval context — the system does not hard-code
recommendations per industry and works for industries not listed here.

## Retail
Common focus areas: demand forecasting, inventory optimisation, personalised
marketing, customer-service automation, supply-chain visibility.

## Banking
Common focus areas: fraud detection, credit-risk scoring, customer onboarding,
anti-money-laundering monitoring, document-heavy back-office automation.

## Healthcare
Common focus areas: claims processing, clinical documentation, patient triage,
appointment scheduling, diagnostic imaging support, readmission risk.

## Manufacturing
Common focus areas: predictive maintenance, quality inspection, production
scheduling, supply-chain optimisation, energy management.

## Education
Common focus areas: automated assessment and grading, personalised learning,
student-success early warning, admissions document processing.

## Insurance
Common focus areas: claims processing, underwriting automation, fraud
detection, policy-document understanding, customer churn prediction.

## Logistics
Common focus areas: route optimisation, demand forecasting, fleet maintenance,
warehouse automation, delivery-time prediction.

## Telecommunications
Common focus areas: churn prediction, network anomaly detection, customer
service automation, billing-document processing, capacity planning.
