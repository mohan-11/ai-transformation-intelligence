# AI Capability Guide

This guide describes generic AI capability patterns that can be applied to any
industry or business process. It is reference knowledge used for retrieval and
reasoning — not a hard-coded recommendation table.

## Predictive Analytics
Forecasting future outcomes (demand, failures, risk, churn, costs) from
historical structured data. Typically requires time-series records and labeled
outcomes. High value where decisions are currently reactive.

## Anomaly Detection
Finding unusual patterns in transaction or sensor streams — fraud, defects,
equipment failure, network intrusion. Requires event logs or telemetry and a
baseline of normal behaviour. Watch for false-positive fatigue.

## Text Classification & NLP
Classifying, routing and extracting meaning from unstructured text: emails,
tickets, reviews, forms. Requires labeled examples for supervised learning.
Strong fit for high-volume intake and triage work.

## Document Understanding
OCR + information extraction to digitise invoices, claims, contracts and forms.
Requires layout/template samples and ground-truth fields. High value in
paper-heavy, compliance-driven processes.

## Conversational AI
Chatbots and assistants that handle routine questions and requests. Requires a
knowledge base, intent definitions and an escalation path to humans.

## Recommendation & Personalisation
Suggesting relevant items, content or next-best-actions from interaction
history. Requires user profiles and behaviour data; benefits from A/B testing.

## Computer Vision
Detecting objects, defects and anomalies in images and video. Requires
annotated datasets and often edge/GPU inference. Strong for visual inspection.

## Process Automation (RPA)
Automating repetitive rule-based tasks across systems. Fits stable, high-volume
manual processes. Requires clear process documentation and system access.

## Optimisation & Scheduling
Mathematical optimisation of routing, scheduling, capacity and allocation.
Requires explicit constraints and objectives. High value in logistics and
resource-heavy operations.

## Generative AI for Content
Drafting summaries, reports, copy and code with LLMs. Accelerates content work
but requires human review to control hallucination and quality.

## Knowledge Management & Search
Semantic search and retrieval over documents so staff find answers quickly.
Requires a document corpus and embedding/vector infrastructure.

## Personalisation Engine
Tailoring an experience or intervention to an individual. Requires consent
management and fairness review to be deployed responsibly.
